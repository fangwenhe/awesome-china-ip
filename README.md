# Awesome China IP 🇨🇳

> The practical, curated resource hub for **China intellectual property**: trademark registration, copyright, patents, brand protection and enforcement — written for foreign brands, creators, startups and developers who sell into China or work with Chinese partners.

**China is a first-to-file jurisdiction. If you don't register first, someone else owns your brand in China.** This list collects everything you need — official portals, search tools, fee schedules, registration guides, enforcement options and case lessons — so you can protect your IP in China without getting lost in translation.

## Why this list exists

- China received **4.206 million trademark registrations in 2025**; valid registered trademarks reached **53.03 million** (+6.5% YoY) — the most active trademark jurisdiction in the world.
- China operates on **first-to-file**: the first applicant owns the mark, regardless of who used it first abroad.
- **Trademark squatting is common** — bad actors monitor foreign brands, register their names in China, then demand payment to transfer.
- Cross-border e-commerce (Amazon, TikTok Shop, AliExpress, Temu) increasingly requires China-side IP proof for brand registry and store appeals.

## Official institutions

- **CNIPA** (China National Intellectual Property Administration, 国家知识产权局) — the national authority for trademarks, patents and geographical indications. → https://www.cnipa.gov.cn
- **China Trademark Office** (商标局, under CNIPA) — trademark examination and registration. → https://sbj.cnipa.gov.cn
- **CNIPA Trademark Search Portal** (中国商标网) — official free search of published & registered marks. → http://wcjs.sbj.cnipa.gov.cn
- **China Copyright Protection Center** (中国版权保护中心, CCPC) — voluntary copyright registration and software copyright (软著) for works distributed in China. → https://www.ccopyright.com.cn
- **Patent Search & Analysis System (CNIPA)** — official patent search (Chinese). → https://pss-system.cponline.cnipa.gov.cn
- **WIPO China Office** — international cooperation, Madrid & PCT guidance. → https://www.wipo.int/en/web/china

## Search & verification tools

- **China Trademark Search (CNIPA)** — official, free, by name/class; the source of truth. → http://wcjs.sbj.cnipa.gov.cn
- **Global Brand Database (WIPO)** — cross-jurisdiction trademark search including China designations. → https://branddb.wipo.int
- **Madrid Monitor (WIPO)** — track international registrations designating China. → https://www.wipo.int/madrid/monitor
- **Business registry lookups** — verify the company behind a mark: [Qichacha 企查查](https://www.qcc.com), [Tianyancha 天眼查](https://www.tianyancha.com), [National Enterprise Credit Info](https://www.gsxt.gov.cn)
- **Copyright verification** — ask the work's registration number at CCPC for authenticity checks.

## Trademark registration in China

- **General guide for foreign brands** — step-by-step: classes, search, filing, examination, registration. See [China Trademark Toolkit](https://github.com/fangwenhe/china-trademark-toolkit) (open-source bilingual toolkit with templates).
- **Official fee**: online application **¥270/class** (线下 ¥300/class); total cost usually a few hundred RMB per class plus agency fees.
- **Timeline**: filing → ~1 month formality check → 3–6 months substantive examination → publication (3 months opposition window) → registration certificate (~3–4 months later). Realistic total: **6–12 months**.
- **Key strategic tips**:
  - Register a **Chinese-character mark** — English-only marks have weak recognition/protection in China.
  - File **before** entering the market (Tmall/Tmall Global, Amazon China, WeChat commerce all require valid marks for brand registry).
  - Cover your **core class + adjacent classes** (defensive filings).
  - Beware **trademark agents that overcharge or squat** — always verify against official fees and use the official search before filing.
- **Rights holders & agents**: foreign companies/individuals can file directly or via a **registered Chinese agency** (备案代理机构). Verify an agency's filing record at CNIPA before engaging.

## Copyright registration in China

- **Why register**: voluntary registration (自愿登记) is the strongest evidence of ownership in China; useful for takedowns on Tmall/Taobao/Douyin, licensing deals, and enforcement.
- **Covered works**: software (软件著作权/软著), literary works, art/design, music, video, photography, and more.
- **How**: CCPC online filing (or via agencies) → ~30–60 working days → certificate. See [China Copyright Registration](https://github.com/fangwenhe/china-copyright-registration) for a practical English guide.
- **Fee note**: software copyright registration is **free** since 2017 policy adjustments for many categories; literary/art works usually carry official fees.

## Patents in China

- **Types**: invention patent (发明, 20 yrs), utility model (实用新型, 10 yrs), industrial design (外观设计, 15 yrs).
- **Key fact**: China grants more patents than any other country; novelty is absolute (worldwide prior art counts).
- **Filing routes**: direct CNIPA filing, PCT national phase, or via patent agencies. **Note**: foreign applicants must use a **registered Chinese patent agency** (专利代理机构) for filings in China — direct foreign self-filing is not allowed.
- **Search**: CNIPA Patent Search System (above) or Google Patents for English abstracts.

## Madrid System & international filings

- **Registering a China mark via Madrid**: designate China in your international application at WIPO — costs roughly CHF 653 + CHF 100 per class for China designation (subject to CNIPA examination like a domestic application).
- **Registering abroad from China**: Chinese applicants can use Madrid to protect marks overseas.
- **Key rule**: Madrid designation **does not** override China's first-to-file rule — the designation date determines priority.

## Brand protection & enforcement

- **Opposition (异议)**: third parties may oppose a published mark within **3 months** of publication.
- **Invalidation (无效宣告)** & **revocation (撤三)**: challenge squatted or unused marks.
- **Enforcement routes**:
  - Administrative: complaint to local **Market Regulation Bureau (市场监管局)** — fast, low cost.
  - Civil: lawsuit in Chinese courts (IP courts in Beijing/Shanghai/Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Hangzhou).
  - Customs: record your mark with **China Customs IP protection** to stop counterfeit imports/exports.
  - Platforms: Tmall/Taobao/TikTok Shop/Douyin IP complaint portals (品牌保护平台).
- **Cost reality**: enforcement usually requires a **registered mark + evidence of use**; keep chain-of-title and use evidence from day one.

## Cross-border e-commerce & IP

- **Amazon Brand Registry China**: requires an active trademark (Chinese or US/other accepted) — China marks help with **transborder protection** (transborder: 跨境保护) for sellers.
- **TikTok Shop & Douyin**: brand verification and IP complaints need valid China marks/copyright.
- **Customs**: record marks to intercept counterfeits at ports.
- **Practical rule**: your **store name, logo, product photos and listing text** all need IP coverage before scaling ads or taking the brand public.

## Case studies & lessons

- **"First-to-file" traps**: foreign brands losing their own names to squatters (e.g., numerous consumer brands, restaurant chains) — the standard remedy is invalidation/opposition, which costs time and money.
- **English mark ≠ protected in China**: many brands register only in their home country and lose the Chinese market; Chinese-character marks are essential.
- **Platform IP complaints**: sellers win takedowns faster with a registered China mark + copyright certificate for listing photos.
